Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how SpendWise handles information when you use this expense-tracking service. The information actually processed can depend on the features enabled and the way the service is hosted.

Information you provide

Information you choose to enter may include account details and expense records, such as amounts, dates, categories, and descriptions. Please avoid entering information that is not needed to manage your expenses, especially sensitive personal or financial account credentials.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide and maintain expense-tracking features, display your records and summaries, respond to support requests, and protect the service from misuse. The current application may not implement all account or data-storage features; hosting and deployment choices can also affect how information is processed.

Storage, sharing, and security

Information may be processed by the service and its hosting provider. It is not shared with others except as needed to operate the service, comply with applicable law, or protect users and the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so do not submit information you would not want processed by the service.

Retention and your choices

Information is retained only as long as needed for the service's purposes or applicable legal requirements. If you have questions about, or want to request access to or deletion of, information you provided, contact the service operator through the channel where you accessed SpendWise.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. For privacy questions, contact the service operator.
